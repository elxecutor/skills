Search Jumia Nigeria directly with:

`https://www.jumia.com.ng/catalog/?q=<URL-encoded search>`

Support complete searches by adding URL query filters requested by the user, such as:

- `sort=lowest-price`, `highest-price`, `rating`, or `newest`
- `rating=4-5`
- `price_discount=30-100`
- `official_store=1`
- `shop_premium_services=shop_express`
- category-specific filters such as `size=26`, `brand=...`, `color_family=...`, `seller_score=4-5`, and `page=2`

Discover valid filter names and values from the category page when needed. URL-encode the search and filter values, combine compatible filters, fetch pages concurrently when useful, and return matching product links and listing details. Use direct public endpoints and do not place orders.
